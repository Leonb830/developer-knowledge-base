# Learning Log

Chronological record of real experiments, reviews, and lessons learned.

When work has actually occurred, append an entry using:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Topic or experiment
- **Goal:** What was being learned or investigated?
- **Activity:** What was implemented, read, or tested?
- **Result:** What worked, failed, or remains unverified?
- **Next step:** One concrete follow-up.
- **Related:** Relative link to a topic or project.
```

Do not generate fictional learning history.
